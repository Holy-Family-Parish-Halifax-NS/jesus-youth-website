# Family Prayer Card (folded wallet card)

Prayer written by Josna chechi, approved by Fr. Daris Cherian. Text: `content/prayer-malayalam.md`.

## Files

- `family-prayer-card-print.pdf`: print file, 2 pages (page 1 = outside, page 2 = inside)
- `preview-outside.png`, `preview-inside.png`: previews

## Sizes

- Flat sheet: 5.5 × 4.25 in, plus 1/8 in bleed on every side (PDF page is 5.75 × 4.5 in)
- Folded: 2.75 × 4.25 in (about 7 × 10.8 cm), fits in a wallet
- Fold: vertical, down the middle

## Printing at Staples

- Print double-sided, **flip on short edge**, then trim to 5.5 × 4.25 in and fold in half (ask them to score it first)
- Paper: thick cardstock (about 14 pt / 100 lb cover), matte finish
- Print one test copy first and check: the front cover lines up behind page 2's left panel, text is clear, colors look right

## Fonts

Noto Serif Malayalam (`assets/fonts/noto-serif-malayalam/`, SIL Open Font License) and Lora.
