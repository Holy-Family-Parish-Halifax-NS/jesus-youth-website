Mother Mary comes to our homes

10-Day Family Rosary · Oct 21 to 30 · 7 PM

An initiative of our Jesus Youth Family Prayer Group
Holy Family Syro-Malabar Catholic Parish, Halifax

October is the month of the Holy Rosary. For 10 evenings, a different family hosts the Rosary at their home, and a blessed statue of Mother Mary travels from home to home. Everyone is welcome to join and pray together.

No need to prepare food. Kattan kaapi or Parle-G at most.
