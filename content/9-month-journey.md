# 9-Month Journey

Each month the Family prayer meeting walks through one topic together.

| Month | Topic | Subtopic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | God's Love, The Foundation of Our Families | God's love as the base of family life |
| 2 | Healing and Forgiveness in the Family | Letting go of hurts, reconciliation |
| 3 | Jesus at the Center of the Home | Making Christ the center of family decisions |
| 4 | Praying Together as a Family | Family prayer habits |
| 5 | The Holy Spirit in Family Life | Guidance, patience, wisdom |
| 6 | The Power of the Eucharist | Attending Holy Mass together |
| 7 | Raising Children in Faith | Passing faith to the next generation |
| 8 | Family as a Mission | Witnessing Christ through family life |
| 9 | Living the Jesus Youth Spirituality as Families | Prayer, Word, Sacraments, Fellowship |
